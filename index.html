Beat and Run points use each boat's own optimal VMG angle at that wind speed (converted from certificate VMG to boat speed). 52°–150° points are boat speed as published directly on the certificate. Tick marks below the slider (4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 kt) are the certificate's actual measured wind speeds; values in between are linearly interpolated.
