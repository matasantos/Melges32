Beat and Run points use each boat's own optimal VMG angle at that wind speed (converted from certificate VMG to boat speed). 52°–150° points are boat speed as published directly on the certificate. Tick marks below the slider (4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 kt) are the certificate's actual measured wind speeds; values in between are linearly interpolated. The wind-speed slider runs 4–18kt; the boat-speed rings are zoomed to a 12kt max (rather than 20kt) to keep this fleet readable. BAMAK is checked by default and always shown, drawn solid and on top of any opponents you add; tap a boat's name (not its checkbox) to isolate it against BAMAK.
